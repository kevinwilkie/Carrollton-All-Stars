Each team may keep up to 5 players. A keeper is assigned to that team, costs come out of its $300 budget, and fills a roster slot. Year 1 cost = last year's price + $5 (flat $5 if undrafted). Year 2 cost = ESPN AVG SALARY (rounded). No player may be kept a third straight year.
